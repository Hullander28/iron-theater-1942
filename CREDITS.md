# Credits – Iron Theater 1942

## Audio
All sound effects and the ambient music are **synthesized live in the browser with the Web Audio API** (`polish.js`, `SFX` module).
There are no recorded or downloaded audio files, so no third-party licences apply. Sounds: dice rattle, rifle fire,
artillery, explosions, aircraft engine, ship horn, tank treads, brass "capital captured" sting, and a quiet ambient pad.

## Map
Geography from **Natural Earth** (public domain): naturalearthdata.com, 1:50m admin-0/admin-1 datasets,
projected and merged into game territories by `geo/build.py`. Textures (paper, ocean) are generated procedurally at runtime.

## Art
Unit sprites are original generated renders in a plastic toy-soldier style, cut out and recoloured per power.

## Networking
PeerJS (MIT licence) loaded from its public CDN for online rooms.

Iron Theater 1942 is an original game inspired by classic WWII strategy board games. It is not affiliated with Hasbro, and it uses none of Hasbro's trademarks or artwork.
